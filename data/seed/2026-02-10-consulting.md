Tuesday afternoon, clear head, decided in daylight for once. A consulting firm offered me a lucrative six-month contract — great money, remote, "help portfolio startups avoid the mistakes you made." Options: (A) take the contract for the cash and cushion, (B) turn it down and protect my recovery plus keep space for my own small projects, (C) do it half-time. I chose B. The money was real but it would have filled the exact hole I'm trying to leave empty on purpose — I'd be selling my burnout as a service. Okafor asked "what would taking it cost you?" and the answer was: the quiet I just fought a year to earn. Turned it down over a calm lunch. No 11pm heroics. Just no.
