A recruiter from a big fintech slid into my inbox. Senior IC, comfortable number, "impact at scale." I deleted it after one line. Big companies are where ambition goes to die. You get a title and a ladder and a manager who schedules your growth into quarterly cycles, and one day you wake up optimizing a checkout funnel for a logo you don't love. Not me. I'd rather burn bright and broke building something mine. Sam agreed, or agreed enough. We're three months from a raise. Why would I trade the arena for a cubicle with a view.
