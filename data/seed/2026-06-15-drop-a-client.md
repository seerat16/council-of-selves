Monday afternoon, daylight, decided with Sam in the room. A demanding enterprise lead wanted a custom build that would've meant nights and weekends for a quarter. Options: (A) decline and stay within our sustainable scope, (B) take it for the revenue and eat the overtime, (C) take it but hire first. I chose A. Eighteen months ago I'd have grabbed it and called the exhaustion "ambition." Now I know the price of that trade in molars and Decembers. The revenue was real; the cost was the exact life I rebuilt. No thanks. Noting the pattern in the good direction: rested morning, someone in the room, a "no" that protects the life. These are the calls that keep aging well.
