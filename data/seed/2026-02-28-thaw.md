Last day of February and I can feel winter loosening its grip on me, not just the weather. I'm not the person who crashed in December and I'm definitely not the one who ran himself into the ground in the fall. Somewhere in the middle a third version is standing up. Okafor asked what he wants and for the first time I had an answer that wasn't about survival: I want to make something again, but on my own terms this time, small and mine and unhurried. Regret about deleting the side project is still there, dull. Maybe that regret is data — maybe the wanting-to-build never actually left, it just needed me to be well enough to hear it. Rated a 3. Ready for spring.
