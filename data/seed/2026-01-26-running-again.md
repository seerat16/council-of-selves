The running is becoming the thing that holds the week together. Not the metrics of it — I don't track pace anymore, that's the point — just the fact of putting shoes on and going out into the cold and coming back a slightly better-organized person. Okafor calls it "a moving meditation you're allowed to be bad at." I'm bad at it and it still works. Vesuvio, a run, one honest conversation a day: that's the whole regimen and it's more effective than the year of hustle that preceded it. Sam and I laughed today, actually laughed, about how unhinged fall-me was. Rated a 3, and it's a real 3, load-bearing.
