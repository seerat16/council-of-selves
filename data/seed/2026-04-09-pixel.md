I adopted a dog. Her name is Pixel — a scruffy gray terrier mix with one ear that refuses to stand up, and she chose me at the shelter by falling asleep on my shoe. Priya says it's the least "grind-me" thing I've ever done and she's right; fall-me would have called a dog a "recurring liability with no ROI." Pixel forces walks, forces mornings, forces me out of my head and onto a sidewalk twice a day. Okafor is delighted — "you got yourself a structure that loves you back." She sleeps under the desk while I build Tiny Ledger. Best impulsive decision of the year and I made it in broad daylight, sober, on a Thursday. Growth.
