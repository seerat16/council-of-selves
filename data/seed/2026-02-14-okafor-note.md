Okafor session notes. She pointed out a pattern I hadn't named: my biggest reversals all happen at night, on Sundays, after weeks I'd rate low. "You make decisions when your nervous system is loudest," she said. The fix isn't more willpower, it's a rule: no irreversible choices after dark on a bad week. Sleep on it, decide in the morning, in daylight, with a person in the room. I told her about unquitting the company on a Wednesday morning and she said "see — morning-you is wiser than you give him credit for." Writing this down because I know future-me will forget it precisely when he needs it most.
