New year and for once no manic list of goals. Okafor asked me to define "enough" and this time I had a sentence: enough is when I can hear my own life over the noise of the work. I'm not there but I know the address now. Health, rest, the people who stayed — Priya, Sam, Jordan — those are the things that actually refill me. Speed was never a value, it was a way to avoid feeling how empty the tank was. Ran a 5k without stopping. Cried at the end, good crying. Went back to work part-time and it held. The company survived me resting, exactly like Jordan said it would. That fact is going to take a while to forgive myself for doubting.
