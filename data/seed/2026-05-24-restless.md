Tiny Ledger has real users now and here comes the familiar itch — the "what's next, go bigger, raise money, hire" voice. Except this time I clock it as a feeling instead of obeying it as an order. Okafor and I talked about the restlessness: it's not wrong, it's just loud, and loud isn't the same as right. Part of me wants to turn this into a Real Company; part of me remembers what Real Company did to my molars. The autonomy is the whole point and scaling usually trades autonomy for growth. Sitting with the question instead of answering it fast. That patience is the actual growth. Rated a 4. Restless, but the useful kind, the kind I now know how to hold.
