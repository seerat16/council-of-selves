Thinking about that consulting contract I turned down in February [2026-02-10] and I'm just — glad. Genuinely glad I did. If I'd taken it I'd have spent the spring debugging other people's startups instead of building Tiny Ledger, and I'd have called the money "freedom" while it quietly bought my time back at a discount. Declining it is what freed me to make this thing. That's the outcome, clear as day: the "no" in daylight was one of the best calls I've made. Funny how the decisions I make rested and in the morning keep aging well, and the midnight ones keep needing to be undone. Someone should tell me that at 11pm on a Sunday. Rated a 4.
